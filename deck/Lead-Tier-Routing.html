THE PROBLEM

Tiering relies on estimates and partial data

01

Self-reported spend

The tier rests on travel spend the customer declares at signup.

02

Missing data

The tier depends on two inputs. One of them is missing for 20 of 43 companies.

03

No second look

Signup data is not re-checked against external research or actual travel.

Every tier meets the rules on paper. The inputs behind it still need verifying and updating.

PRINCIPLES

Design rules

Recommendations, not decisions

A person makes every tier change.

Flags need evidence

Missing data triggers research, not a recommendation.

Explained and sourced

Every recommendation cites its sources, green included.

Rules for numbers, AI for context

Rules check the hard numbers; AI weighs what rules can’t see.

AI outputs are verified

Quotes, links and numbers are checked in code.

Every change is tested

A change is kept only if it improves the results.
