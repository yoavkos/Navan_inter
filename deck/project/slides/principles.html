PRINCIPLES

Design rules

Recommendations, not decisions

A person makes every tier change.

Flags need evidence

Missing data triggers research, not a recommendation.

Explained and sourced

Every recommendation cites its sources, green included.

Rules for numbers, AI for context

Rules check the hard numbers; AI weighs what rules can’t see.

AI outputs are verified

Quotes, links and numbers are checked in code.

Every change is tested

A change is kept only if it improves the results.
